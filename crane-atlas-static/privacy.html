A static copy of the final public-facing privacy structure from the retired application.

At a glance

Crane Atlas was built as a private Nest-based service.

Account details, Journey content, Notebook writing and operational security information were handled only to provide and protect the service.

1. Who we are

Crane Atlas was an independently operated private web application.

2. Information we handle

Account identifiers, Nest membership, user-created Memories, Review Items, Notebook entries and service security records.

3. How and why we use information

Information was processed to provide accounts, private shared spaces, recovery, email verification and abuse protection.

4. Sharing and international transfers

No advertising profiles or public social graph were part of Crane Atlas.

5. Cookies and device features

Session cookies and browser capabilities supported authenticated use and optional media features.

6. Retention and deletion

Removed content used short recovery windows before deletion.

7. Your rights and choices

Users could manage their account and content through the application.

8. Security

The final build included password hashing, session controls, rate limits and email verification safeguards.
